B Set up your own shipment in 4 steps

Everything is done in the admin console (log in with ADMIN_USERNAME / ADMIN_PASSWORD from your .env file).

  1. 1
    Create the shipment

    Admin → Shipments → New shipment. Fill in the sender, receiver (name + email) and origin/destination, then Create. You get a tracking number like {{ business.tracking_example }}, and the receiver is emailed automatically.

  2. 2
    Add checkpoints as the parcel moves

    Open the shipment → Add checkpoint. Pick a status (e.g. Picked Up, Arrived at Hub), type a place like Lyon, France, add a remark. It appears on the timeline and map, and the receiver gets an email.

  3. 3
    Optional — make the live map move

    In the Live journey panel: Step 1 type origin, any stops and destination → Build route. Step 2 set departure and arrival times. Step 3 press Start. The marker now moves along the route on its own. Use Pause / Resume for delays and Arrived at the end.

  4. 4
    Share the tracking number

    Customers enter it on the homepage (or open /track/<number>). The page refreshes itself every 30 seconds. Customers with an account see every shipment sent to their email right here.
