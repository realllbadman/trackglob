Our story

Built for people who just want to know where their parcel is

We started with a simple idea: tracking should be easy to read and never leave you guessing. Every shipment we handle gets its own tracking page with a map, a step-by-step progress bar and a complete checkpoint history entered by our operations team.

Today we serve individuals and businesses with domestic road delivery, express air, ocean freight and customs support — and we still answer the phone ourselves.

What we deliver

One tracking experience across every service.

Domestic road delivery

Door-to-door ground delivery across the country, with hub-by-hub scans and scheduled route maps.

Express air

Priority handling for urgent documents and parcels on the fastest available routes.

Ocean freight

Consolidated sea freight for heavier and bulkier consignments at a lower cost per kilo.

Customs clearance

Guidance on commercial invoices and declarations so international parcels clear borders smoothly. Any official duties are assessed by the destination country's customs authority.

Warehousing

Secure short-term storage and consolidation at partner hubs.

Business accounts

Scheduled collections and a single dashboard for every parcel your team sends.
