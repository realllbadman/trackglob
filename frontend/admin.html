Quick start — how to track a shipment click to show/hide
  1. Create — open New shipment below, fill in sender, receiver name + email, origin and destination, press Create. Copy the tracking number it gives you.
  2. Update — press Open on the shipment, then Add checkpoint: choose a status and type a place ("City, Country"). The receiver is emailed each time.
  3. Live map (optional) — in Live journey: Step 1 build the route, Step 2 set departure + arrival, Step 3 press Start. The marker moves by itself; use Pause/Resume for delays and Arrived at the end.
  4. Share — send the tracking number to your customer. They track it on the homepage; the page refreshes every 30 s.
